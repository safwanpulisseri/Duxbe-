### **Privacy Policy**

**Last Updated: April 8, 2025**

Duxbe Business (“we,” “our,” or “us”) values your privacy and is committed to
protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we
collect, use, and protect your data when you use the Duxbe Business app (“the
App”).

### **1\. Information We Collect**

We may collect the following types of information when you use the App:

- **Personal Information**: Name, email address, phone number, business details,
  and other contact information.

- **Transactional Information**: Order history, purchases, payment details, and
  other transaction-related data.

- **Device Information**: Information about your device, including device type,
  operating system version, unique identifiers (such as device ID), and usage
  data.

### **2\. How We Use Your Information**

We use your information for the following purposes:

- To provide, personalize, and improve our services.

- To process orders and payments.

- To communicate with you regarding transactions, updates, and promotions (if
  you opt-in).

- To analyze usage patterns and improve the user experience of the App.

- To comply with legal obligations.

### **3\. Account Deletion and Data Retention**

- **Account Deletion**: If you choose to delete your account, we will remove
  your personal information from our active database. However, your transaction
  history and previous orders will remain stored in our records for historical
  and legal purposes, but they will be marked as “inactive.”

- **Data Retention**: We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide
  services and comply with legal requirements. Transaction data will be retained
  for record-keeping and audit purposes, even if your account is deleted.

### **4\. Sharing of Information**

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. However, we may share your
data with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate the App,
process payments, and deliver services (e.g., payment gateways). These third
parties are obligated to protect your data and use it only for the purposes of
providing services to us.

### **5\. Security of Your Data**

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including
encryption and secure data storage practices. However, no system can be
completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of your data.

### **6\. User Rights**

You have the right to access, update, and delete your personal information. If
you wish to exercise these rights, please contact us at the contact information
provided below.

### **7\. Changes to Privacy Policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we
will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this policy. We encourage you
to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your
data.
